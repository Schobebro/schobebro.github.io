Help with Claret, your purchase and your study data, and how to reach us.

Contact

For help with Claret, or to report a mistake in a lesson or question, e-mail either of us:

Leo: leoschoberwalter@gmail.com

Felix: [[SECONDARY_SUPPORT_EMAIL]]

We both look after Claret and also answer privacy questions. If you can, include the course version shown in About and the title of the lesson or question. Please do not send payment details, passwords or Apple Account details.

The quickest way to report a content error is in the app: Settings, then About, then Report an error by e-mail. It writes the e-mail for you and adds the course and app versions.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings in the app, choose About and then Restore purchase. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Restoring never charges anything. If it does not restore, e-mail us with the app version and the message you see. Purchases are handled by Apple; we cannot access your payment details.

Restoring brings back the Full Course, not your study history. Your progress is stored on your device and is not kept by us.

Your progress

Your answers, reviews, mock results and settings are stored in the app on your device. Deleting the app deletes them, and we cannot recover them. Your device's own backups may include them, under your Apple settings.

To start again, open Settings and choose Reset progress. This deletes mastery, answers, reviews, mock results and your streak; your exam date, reminders and purchase stay.
