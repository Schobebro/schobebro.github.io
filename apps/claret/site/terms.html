Free lessons and the Full Course

The app is free to download and includes lessons, practice and one mock exam you can use without paying. The Full Course is a single in-app purchase that unlocks all lessons, drills, maps and more mock exams. It is not a subscription and does not renew.

Apple sells the purchase and processes the payment under its own terms. The App Store shows the price in your local currency before you confirm. Refunds are requested from Apple.

To restore the Full Course, for example after reinstalling or on another device with the same Apple Account, open Settings, then About, then Restore purchase. Restoring never charges anything. It restores the purchase, not your study history.

A study aid, not a guarantee

Claret is written for WSET® Level 2 candidates. It helps you prepare with lessons, practice questions, a tasting trainer and timed mock exams. It does not guarantee that you pass any exam. The readiness shown in the app is a guide, not a forecast of your result. Check the current requirements with your course provider.

The tasting trainer teaches the method and its vocabulary; it is not preparation for a tasting exam at a higher level. The labels in the course are fictional. The maps contain information from OpenStreetMap, which is made available under the Open Database License.

We take care to keep the course accurate. If you find a mistake, please tell us so that we can correct it.

Your progress stays on your device

Your progress is saved automatically in the app on your device. There is no account, no cloud sync of our own and no export. Your device's backups may include app data. We have no remote access to your progress and cannot restore it. Details are in the privacy policy.
