Joint responsibility

We decide together about this website and support. We share all data protection tasks: information, security, handling requests, deletion and dealing with data breaches. Neither of us is solely responsible for any of them.

This is the essence of our arrangement on joint controllership under Art. 26 GDPR. You can exercise your rights against either of us; we coordinate the handling between us.

Data in the app

Claret works offline and has no account. It has no analytics, advertising or tracking and contains no third-party SDK. The app does not collect personal data and does not send your study activity to us.

Your answers, progress, review schedule, mock results, exam date and settings are stored only in the app on your device. Reminders are local notifications, scheduled on the device; the app asks for permission only when you switch them on. The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

We cannot read, change or delete this data remotely. Settings, then Reset progress, deletes your study records on the device. Deleting the app deletes its local data under the rules of the operating system.

Device backups

The app does not synchronise your progress with a server. Your device's own backups, for example to iCloud or a computer, may include app data. That depends on your settings and the rules of the backup service. Deleting the app does not delete existing backups.

Purchases

The Full Course is an in-app purchase handled by Apple through StoreKit. Apple processes the payment and your payment information. The app only asks Apple whether the course has been bought on your Apple Account, to decide whether to unlock it. We do not receive your name, e-mail address or payment details through the app. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by its own privacy policy.

App Store and crash reports

Apple separately processes data in connection with the App Store and iOS and iPadOS. If you have agreed in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass us crash reports and aggregated usage information. That gives us no access to your study data.

Apple explains this in App Store & Privacy and App Analytics & Privacy. You manage sharing in your device's settings under Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

When you write to us

If you e-mail us, we process your e-mail address, your message and any attachments you choose to send, to answer you and to investigate your report or correct the course. We receive this data only if you send it. Contacting us is voluntary and uses your own e-mail service outside the app. Please send no sensitive information and no unnecessary personal data.

Right to object

Where we process data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you can object on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). We will assess your objection under the legal requirements.

Contact us at either address above. For our locations you can contact in particular the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information or the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg. Other supervisory authorities competent under Art. 77 GDPR can also receive complaints. You manage the study data stored only in the app directly on your iPhone or iPad.

We do not use your contact or website data for automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects, and we do not profile you.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and the app's privacy label in the App Store first.
